## Overview
Dino Arena brings competitive gameplay to the familiar infinite runner mechanics. Unlike the original single-player experience, you are not alone in the desert. You will compete against other players simultaneously on the exact same track, seeing their progress as "ghosts" in real-time while the speed constantly increases. 

## B2B & Platform Integration
Dino Arena is designed as a B2B mini-game that can be seamlessly embedded into any existing website, app, or community platform to drive user engagement and retention.
* **Cross-Platform Support:** Fully compatible with modern web browsers, desktop clients, and mobile applications.
* **Competitive Matchmaking:** Players can join ranked or casual lobbies. The last dinosaur running takes the victory.
* **Engagement & Retention:** Designed to increase average session lengths and provide a fun, interactive social layer for your user base.
* **Custom UI & Branding:** The game UI can be fully customized to match your platform's branding, color palette, and design system.

## Infrastructure & Self-Hosting
We exclusively support self-hosted architecture for enterprise and platform partners. You retain 100% control over your hosting, player data, user authentication, and matchmaking logic without relying on third-party game servers.

## How to Play
1. **Join a Lobby**: Create an account or play as a guest, then choose your preferred game mode.
2. **Enter the Arena**: Customize your username and jump into the matchmaking queue.
3. **Live Multiplayer**: Watch your opponents running in real-time as red ghosts. Everyone faces the exact same obstacles at the exact same time.
4. **Avoid Obstacles**: Jump over cacti and duck under Pterodactyls. The game speed increases the further you run.
5. **Win**: The last dinosaur running wins the match and climbs the global rankings.

## Game Modes
* **Practice Mode**: A casual mode to enjoy the game, race against others, and hone your skills without affecting your rank.
* **1v1 Duel**: A high-stakes head-to-head battle for survival.
* **4-Player FFA**: A four-player free-for-all match.
* **8-Player Battle Royale**: The ultimate test of endurance against seven other players for maximum leaderboard points.

## Arena Rules
* **Real-Time Interaction**: Opponents appear as red ghosts. You cannot physically collide with them, but you can track their position relative to yours.
* **Sudden Death**: Hitting any obstacle results in immediate elimination from the round.
* **Winner Takes All**: Only the final survivor is awarded the victory for the match.
